Our Services

Comprehensive care for every stage of life

From early childhood through adulthood, we provide compassionate, professional care tailored to meet the unique needs of each individual and family we serve.

DAYCARE

A safe place to play, learn, and make friends.

Our daycare program provides a nurturing environment where children can explore, learn, and grow. We focus on early childhood development through play-based learning, social interaction, and age-appropriate activities.

Monday - Friday, 6:30 AM - 6:30 PM
Ages: 6 weeks - 5 years
  • Age-appropriate curriculum for children 6 weeks to 5 years
  • Low child-to-staff ratios for personalized attention
  • Nutritious meals and snacks included
  • Daily outdoor play and physical activities
  • Early literacy and numeracy development
  • Art, music, and creative expression
  • Regular progress reports and parent communication

SPECIAL NEEDS CARE

Support that meets your child where they are.

Our special needs program provides individualized care and support for children with diverse abilities. Our trained staff work closely with families to create personalized care plans that celebrate progress and foster independence.

Monday - Friday, 6:30 AM - 6:30 PM
All ages welcome
  • Individualized Education Plans (IEPs)
  • Trained staff in special needs care
  • Sensory-friendly environments
  • Therapeutic activities and exercises
  • Behavioral support strategies
  • Collaboration with therapists and specialists
  • Inclusive activities with peer interaction

ADULT DAY SERVICES

Engaging days for adults. Peace of mind for families.

Our adult day services provide a supportive community for older adults and individuals with disabilities. We offer engaging activities, wellness support, and social connection in a dignified, caring environment.

Monday - Friday, 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Adults 18+
  • Social activities and group programs
  • Health and wellness monitoring
  • Nutritious meals and snacks
  • Memory care activities
  • Physical exercise and therapy
  • Transportation assistance available
  • Respite for family caregivers
